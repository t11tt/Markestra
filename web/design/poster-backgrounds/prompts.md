# Poster background sources

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Original PNGs are in this directory; optimized WebP assets used by the app are in `../../public/poster-backgrounds/`.

All prompts request opaque portrait 3:4 photographic poster backgrounds with quiet headline and product-photo zones. Typography, prices and merchant photos are composed by the application; the backgrounds contain no product claims.
